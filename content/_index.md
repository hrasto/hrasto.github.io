I recently completed my PhD at [JADS](https://www.jads.nl) / [Tilburg University](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu), supervised by [Emmanuel Keuleers](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/staff/e-a-keuleers).
My research sits at the intersection of NLP and psycholinguistics — I work on tokenization, language model evaluation, corpus statistics, and the conditions under which models generalise across domains and registers.

I am currently open to new opportunities. I am equally interested in continuing NLP research (generalisability, input representation, language codecs, domain drift, quantitative/theoretical/psycholinguistics) and in pivoting to adjacent fields such as robotics, energy grid management and load prediction, accessible language technologies, or niche R&D more broadly.
